# Chocolate Sales Dashboard


A sales dashboard for a chocolate business with 22 products, sold in countries including the USA, UK, India, Australia and New Zealand.

## The problem
The business needed a quick way to see which products sell best, how sales change by month and which sales people perform strongest.

## What you can explore
- Total sales amount (6.18M), boxes shipped (177K) and number of products (22)
- Sales by product, by month and by country (map)
- Average sale amount per sales person
- A product slicer to filter everything

## What I found
- Top products are Smooth Sliky Salty (0.35M), 50% Dark Bites (0.34M), White Choc (0.33M), Peanut Butter Cubes (0.32M) and Eclairs (0.31M).
- January is the strongest month (0.90M). Sales dip in February and April, then recover in June (0.87M).
- The top sales person by average sale is Madelene Upcott (7.0K), followed by Ches Bonnell (6.7K) and Oby Sorrel (6.5K).
